# Dolce & Grokbana

Install one Grok Bot, give it your company's website, and it builds a 4-bot team that runs your merch from start to finish: brand, mockups, vendor pricing, quotes, orders, shipping and customer replies. Nothing ships, sends or spends without your OK, and anything over $500 is blocked.

- **The full spec, machine-readable:** [`dolce-and-grokbana.bot.json`](dolce-and-grokbana.bot.json)
- **Architecture, the team and the rules:** [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Template card text:** [`template.md`](template.md)
- **Worked example, a cold run on galatiq.ai:** [`../verticals/clothing-brand/examples/galatiq/`](../verticals/clothing-brand/examples/galatiq/)
