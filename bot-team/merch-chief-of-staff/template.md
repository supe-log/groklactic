# Merch Chief of Staff (Grok Bot marketplace template)

**Description:** Add one bot and get a whole merch team. Merch Chief of Staff reads your company's site and builds sub-bots that design, source, quote and sell branded clothing from start to finish. Every order and customer message waits for your OK.

**Getting started skill:** `merch-team-setup`. **Trigger:** "Build my merch team".

**Plan source:** the public repo `supe-log/groklactic`, branch `bot-team`, files `bot-team/clothing-team.json` and `verticals/clothing-brand/`. No web app or login needed.

**Team it builds (6):** Brand Chief of Staff, Design Studio, Vendor Scout, Outreach & Negotiation, Launch & Growth, Ops & Retro. All routines start paused.

**Founder approval:** orders over $500, refunds and discounts, new vendors, and public use of the logo. Payments run in Stripe test mode only, and messages are draft-only.

**Worked examples:** `examples/no-thought.brand-profile.json` (a founder's own brand) and `examples/galatiq/` (a cold run on the hackathon host, with brand, mockups, vendors, quote request and purchase order).
